## 3. Body

```
The Producer Price Index (PPI) measures the prices U.S. businesses receive for what they sell. It is often called wholesale inflation. In the 12 months to October 2026, it rose 6.5%. Here are six things that stood out to us.

1. Wholesale prices rose 6.5%, the largest 12-month increase since November 2022.

2. The rate rose from 6.3% in September to 6.5%. Long-distance trucking added the most to that change, and several of the biggest moves, up and down, were the margins stores and wholesalers keep.

3. Long-distance trucking prices rose 28.8%, the highest of all 197 months on record. The earlier high was 26.9% in May 2022.

4. System software prices fell 18.7%, the lowest of the 42 months BLS has published, and took more off the 12-month change than any other item.

5. Fuels added 1.92 points, 29.5% of the total and the most since July 2022. Diesel fuel rose 115.2%, and jet fuel rose 106.5%.

6. Printed circuit assemblies and modules rose 155.7%, the largest increase of any final demand item with its own index. Computer storage devices rose 80.4%.

The full report has the seven charts that tell the story, plus the updated PPI Scuba Diver, now through October 2026, so you can explore every item yourself.
```
