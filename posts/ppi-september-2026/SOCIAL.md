Charts in the thread (same as the post):
- `01-headline.png`: Line and bar chart titled Wholesale prices rose 6.3% over the 12 months to September 2026. The 12-month change in PPI final demand from October 2016 to September 2026 runs near 2% to 3% until 2020, dips below zero in 2020, peaks at 11.7% in March 2022, falls to about 0% in mid-2023, and climbs again in 2026 to 6.3% in September. Bars under the line show goods and services each adding a large share; construction adds little.
- `02-waterfall.png`: A waterfall chart of what added up to wholesale inflation in September 2026. Regular gasoline, up 66.1%, added 0.76 percentage points. Long-distance trucking added 0.45, portfolio management 0.42, diesel fuel (up 115.2%) 0.39, other goods wholesaling 0.35, airline passenger services 0.19, hospital inpatient care 0.19 and premium gasoline 0.18. The other 811 items added 3.75. Gas station margins, car dealer margins, eggs and pork pulled it down. The total is 6.35.
- `03-jet-fuel.png`: Line chart titled Jet fuel and airline fares, 12-month price change, October 2016 to September 2026. Jet fuel swings widely, from about minus 68% in 2020 to plus 170% in 2021 and plus 132% in 2022, falls below zero through most of 2023 to 2025, and climbs in 2026 to plus 104.4% in September. Airline fares in the CPI move in a much narrower range, between about minus 29% and plus 43%, and end at plus 22.4%. The fares line has a gap at October 2025.
- `05-portfolio.png`: Line chart titled Portfolio management fees and the stock market, 12-month change, October 2016 to September 2026. Portfolio management (PPI) and the S&P 500, shifted one month, rise and fall together. The S&P 500 swings more widely, from about minus 19% to plus 54%, while portfolio management runs from about minus 14% to plus 33%. In September 2026 portfolio management is up 21.2%, and the S&P 500 was up 19.0% over the 12 months to the end of August.
