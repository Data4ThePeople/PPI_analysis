## Instagram

Media: `social/ppi-scuba-diver-diver.mp4`. Written for followers with no background in economic data. Links in captions are not clickable, so it points to the link in bio; put the post URL there.

```
This scuba diver is carrying 10 years of prices.

Every month, the U.S. government tracks the prices American businesses get for what they sell, everything from gasoline to trucking. It's called the Producer Price Index, or PPI. Think of it as wholesale prices.

We turned it into a dive:

🔴 Red anchors are things that got more expensive. They pull the diver deeper.
🔵 Blue floats are things that got cheaper. They lift the diver up.

The deeper the diver goes, the faster prices are rising.

Watch it sink to 11.7% in March 2022. In September 2026 it sits at 6.3%, its deepest since December 2022. The biggest anchor right now is gasoline, up 66.1% from a year earlier.

Every bubble is a real item, about 820 of them. Tap the link in our bio to dive in yourself.

#inflation #economics #dataviz #prices
```
